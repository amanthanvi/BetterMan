# Keyboard shortcuts

## Reach

Press `?` for the shortcuts dialog. On a man page, the pager keys (`f`, `n`, `g`, `G`, and the section keys) act on the manual.

## Spec

`nextjs/e2e/keyboard.spec.ts`

## Proves

Single-key shortcuts do nothing while the shortcuts dialog, the command palette, or the reading preferences drawer has focus. On a man page, pager keys open find, move through matches, and jump between sections. On a man-page 404, `/` goes to search.
