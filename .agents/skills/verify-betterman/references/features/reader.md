# Man page reader

## Reach

Open `/man/tar/1`, or follow a search result, a short URL, or a palette hit. The seeded page checklist and the count comparison are `.agents/skills/render-man-page/SKILL.md`.

## Spec

`nextjs/e2e/man.spec.ts`

`nextjs/e2e/reader.spec.ts`

## Proves

tar(1) shows the heading tar(1), a contents list, and Find in page. Find reports a match count and moves between matches. Reading preferences can switch the distro and keep the URL hash. `/man/gunzip/1` ends on `/man/gzip/1`. `/man/printf` offers printf(1) and printf(3).
